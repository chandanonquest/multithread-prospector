## 2. Company Scenario & Strategic Fit
### The Macro Scenario
Kelsey-Seybold is one of the nation's premier multi-specialty physician practices and an accredited Accountable Care Organization. Operating 40+ multi-specialty centers in Houston, their clinicians handle hundreds of thousands of patient visits annually inside an enterprise Epic environment. With physicians managing tight 15–20 minute appointment slots, documentation drag is the #1 threat to physician satisfaction and operational capacity.

### Why Commure is a Perfect Fit (The Value Wedge)
- **Current Operational Reality**: Physicians manually type or dictate into Epic EHR note templates between visits, spending 1.5–2 hours each evening completing charts ("pajama time").
- **The Scaling Friction**: As new clinics open, recruiting and retaining top physicians becomes harder if administrative burdens remain high. Delayed chart closure also stalls billing cycles and increases downcoding.
- **Our Economic Wedge**: Commure Ambient AI Scribe listens to natural patient-physician dialogue, structures clinical notes directly into Epic fields, and cuts charting time by up to 70%—freeing 2+ hours daily per physician and accelerating revenue cycle capture.
