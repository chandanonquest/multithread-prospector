# Account Qualification & Fit Framework

This framework prioritizes **Company-First Qualification** before individual contact outreach. Target accounts must satisfy situational suitability criteria before prospect discovery begins.

---

## 1. Five Dimensions of Account Suitability

### A. Business Model & Growth Trajectory
- **Primary Business Engine**: B2B SaaS, HealthTech, FinTech, Enterprise Infrastructure, etc.
- **Headcount Velocity**: 6-month & 12-month growth rates. High hiring in relevant functional departments signals active budget and acute scaling bottlenecks.

### B. The "Why Us" Value Hypothesis
- **Current Operational Reality**: How do they currently solve the problem? (In-house scripts, legacy tooling, manual human effort).
- **The Scaling Friction**: What breaks as transaction/clinical/customer volume doubles? Where is cost, revenue leakage, or burnout occurring?
- **Economic Value Wedge**: Tangible metric impact (e.g. 70% documentation time reduction, 35% cloud waste eliminated, 30% faster sales cycle).

### C. Technographic Maturity
- **Prerequisite Tech Stack**: Systems they must run (e.g. Epic EHR, Salesforce, AWS EKS, Snowflake).
- **Complementary Ecosystem**: Tools indicating operational modernization.
- **Competitors / Legacy Tooling**: Software they are outgrowing or struggling to maintain.

### D. Buying Triggers & High-Intent Signals
- **Organizational Triggers**: New VP/Director/C-Suite hire in the last 90 days.
- **Financial Triggers**: Recent funding round, acquisition, or geographic expansion.
- **Strategic Triggers**: System migration, compliance mandate, or public executive initiative.

---

## 2. Account Scoring Gate

- **8.0–10.0 (Tier 1 Bullseye)**: Confirmed compatible stack, high hiring growth, undeniable operational friction. -> *Proceed to Company Dossier & Multi-Thread Ladder.*
- **5.0–7.9 (Tier 2 Nurture)**: Compatible firmographics but missing immediate buying trigger or confirmed tech prerequisites.
- **< 5.0 (Disqualified)**: Low fit, legacy stack with high switching cost, or declining headcount.
