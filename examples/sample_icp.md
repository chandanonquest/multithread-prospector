# Ideal Customer Profile (ICP): CloudMetric AI

## 1. Product & Value Architecture
- **Product**: Autonomous Cloud Cost & Latency Optimizer
- **Core Value Proposition**: Real-time AI Kubernetes and cloud resource optimization reducing AWS/GCP bills by 35% without downtime.
- **Economic Impact**: Recovers \$120k–\$400k in annual cloud waste within 30 days of agent deployment.

## 2. Firmographics & Target Boundaries
- **Industry Verticals**: B2B SaaS, Data Platforms, FinTech, Developer Tools.
- **Headcount**: 50–500 employees (Mid-Market / High-Growth Scaleups).
- **Geography**: United States & Canada.
- **Business Model**: Subscription software, data-intensive APIs.

## 3. Technographics & Stack Requirements
- **Prerequisite Tech**: Kubernetes (EKS, GKE, AKS), Terraform, Prometheus, Datadog.
- **Complementary**: Grafana, ArgoCD, Helm.

## 4. Buyer Personas
- **Economic Buyer**: VP of Engineering / CTO (owns infrastructure budget and engineering velocity).
- **Champion**: Director of DevOps / Head of Infrastructure (feels the daily pain of resource exhaustion and alert fatigue).

## 5. Buying Triggers
- Scaling Kubernetes clusters post-Series B funding.
- Public migration from single cloud to multi-cloud or cost reduction mandates.
- High job posting volume for DevOps / Site Reliability Engineers.
