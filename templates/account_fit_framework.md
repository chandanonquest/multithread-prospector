# Account Qualification & Fit Framework

This framework prioritizes **Company-First Qualification** before any individual stakeholder outreach. A company must meet the suitability criteria before contact discovery begins.

---

## 1. Company Situation & Fit Analysis

Evaluate the target company across five critical dimensions:

### A. Business Model & Growth Trajectory
- **Primary Business Engine**: B2B SaaS, Marketplace, FinTech, Cloud/Infra, HealthTech, etc.
- **Current Scaling Phase**: Seed/Product-Market Fit (1–15), Growth/Ramping GTM (16–50), Scaleup (51–250), Mid-Market (251–1000), Enterprise (1000+).
- **Headcount Velocity**: 6-month & 12-month headcount growth rates. High hiring in relevant departments signals expanding budget and operational strain.

### B. The "Why Us" Value Hypothesis
- **Current Operational Reality**: How do they currently solve the problem? (In-house scripts, legacy tooling, manual human effort, spreadsheets).
- **Operational Strain Point**: What breaks as they double in size? Where is cycle time, revenue leakage, or engineering burnout happening?
- **Economic Value Wedge**: The tangible impact (e.g., 30% faster sales cycles, $150k saved cloud compute, 50% fewer manual triage hours).

### C. Technographic Maturity
- **Core Prerequisite Stack**: Technologies they must have installed (e.g., Salesforce, Kubernetes, Snowflake, HubSpot).
- **Complementary Ecosystem**: Tools that show modern engineering/GTM maturity.
- **Competitor / Legacy Tools**: What tool are they outgrowing or replacing?

### D. Buying Triggers & High-Intent Signals
- **Organizational Triggers**: New VP/Director hire in the last 90 days (new leaders bring new tooling budgets).
- **Financial Triggers**: Recent funding round, secondary financing, or aggressive headcount ramp.
- **Product / Strategic Triggers**: New product line launch, geographic expansion, or migration project.

---

## 2. Company Fit Scoring Gate

Rate the account on a 1–10 Scale:
- **8–10 (High Priority / Tier 1 Account)**: Clear trigger, confirmed compatible stack, high hiring growth, undeniable operational friction. -> *Proceed to Multi-Thread Stakeholder Discovery.*
- **5–7 (Tier 2 Account)**: Compatible firmographics but lacking immediate trigger or verified tech stack. -> *Nurture or test light wedge.*
- **< 5 (Disqualified)**: Poor fit, legacy architecture with high switching cost, or declining headcount. -> *Do not prospect.*
