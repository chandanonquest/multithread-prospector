# Research, Hooks & Conversation Bits Framework

This framework governs the research extraction and outreach generation for each lead.

---

## 1. Research Bullet Points (Facts & Signals)
Extract 3–5 sharp, specific, and verifiable facts:
1. **Recent Company Event**: Latest funding, key hire, product release, or strategic milestone.
2. **Individual Milestone / Focus**: Recent promotion, article/post written, podcast quote, or tenure.
3. **Operational Reality**: Tech stack in use, team size, current open job postings in their org.
4. **Relevant Friction / Opportunity**: Specific challenge common to companies at their current stage.

---

## 2. Personalized Cold Hooks
A 1–2 sentence opener that avoids superficial compliments ("I love your work") and focuses on operational reality:
- **Formula 1 (Trigger Event)**: *"Saw your team recently announced [milestone/product]. Given [associated operational challenge], curious how you're handling [specific workflow]..."*
- **Formula 2 (Role & Tech Stack)**: *"Noticed your team is scaling [technology/infrastructure] alongside [team growth]. Most [titles] we talk to at this stage hit a wall with [specific bottleneck]..."*
- **Formula 3 (Thought Leadership/Public Post)**: *"Caught your recent post regarding [specific insight]. Your point about [nuance] resonated—especially given how [market shift] is impacting [metric]..."*

---

## 3. Conversation Bits & Soundbites
Snappy, memorable one-liners designed for live phone calls, LinkedIn voice notes, or follow-up banter:
- **The Contrast Soundbite**: *"Most teams spend 15 hours a week duct-taping X to Y when they could automate Z in minutes."*
- **The Metric Anchor**: *"We helped [similar company] shave 40% off their deployment cycles without touching their existing CI pipeline."*
- **The Peer Perspective**: *"Every VP of Engineering in the 100–300 employee bracket tells us the same thing about tech debt right now."*

---

## 4. Cold Outreach Draft Structure
- **Subject**: 3–5 words, lowercase, non-promotional (e.g., `quick question on revops stack`, `infra scaling at acme`).
- **Hook**: The personalized opener from above.
- **Bridge**: The single biggest value lever tailored to their role.
- **Social Proof**: 1 sentence metric-backed example.
- **Low-Friction CTA**: Open-ended, zero commitment (e.g., *"Open to a brief idea-swap next week, or is this already solved on your side?"*).
